The Forgotten Hour

Why your mind never powers down

For thousands of years, every culture on earth closed the day with a ritual — a fire tended, a chant, water poured, a single gesture before rest.

Modern life erased that hour. We replaced it with screens, news and noise — and your nervous system never receives the signal to power down. So the mind keeps running long after your head hits the pillow.
